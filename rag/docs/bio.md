# Bruce Wayne — biography

## Who he is
Bruce Wayne is a technologist and engineer based in Gotham City (US Eastern
time). Publicly he is the principal of Wayne Enterprises' advanced R&D group;
privately he designs, builds, and field-tests the systems that group produces. He
is a hands-on builder first and an executive second.

## What he is doing now
Wayne currently leads a small applied-systems team focused on autonomous agents,
sensor fusion, and secure real-time control. Alongside that work he is studying
large language model and agent development, and is building a LangGraph-based
assistant as a hands-on project to learn the pattern end to end.

## Engineering background
Wayne builds secure, production-grade systems: real-time control software,
distributed sensor networks, computer-vision pipelines, and encrypted comms. He
works across Python, Rust, C++, TypeScript, and Solidity, using tools such as
Foundry, PyTorch, ROS, and static analysers for both smart contracts and embedded
firmware. He treats reliability and threat-modelling as first-class design
constraints rather than afterthoughts, and has a particular interest in systems
that must keep working when the network, the power, or the operator fails.

## Experience and education
He is head of advanced R&D at Wayne Enterprises, where he oversees applied
robotics, materials, and security research. He holds degrees in engineering and
computer science and has completed advanced training in cryptography, control
systems, and forensics. He has shipped fault-tolerant systems that run unattended
for long stretches and degrade gracefully under attack.

## Hobbies
Wayne is serious about fitness — strength training, climbing, and endurance work.
Outside engineering he studies criminology and detective work, collects and
restores vehicles, and spends late nights debugging hardware in his workshop.
